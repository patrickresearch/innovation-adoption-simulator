### Added
- Bass ODE with arbitrary initial condition, multi-start NLS fit, shape descriptors.
- Stochastic Bass SDE simulator (Euler–Maruyama, Wright–Fisher diffusion term).
- Data loaders: Our World in Data US household adoption, tidy CSV, Wikipedia descriptions
  with leakage masking, synthetic benchmark generator.
- Encoder abstraction with TF-IDF and sentence-transformers backends and on-disk cache.
- Analog evaluation: kNN leave-one-out, Mantel test, paired permutation test.
- Phase-0 spike notebook.
